Lasagna Recipe

Home

Description

Lasagna is a classic Italian baked dish made by stacking wide, flat pasta sheets with savory meat or tomato sauce, cheese, and sometimes béchamel. This comforting meal is baked until bubbling and golden on top.

Ingredients

  • Lasagna noodles
  • Bolognese sauce (or marinara)
  • Ricotta cheese (or béchamel sauce)
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Parmesan cheese
  • Egg
  • Garlic and onions
  • Italian seasoning

Steps

  1. Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Boil the lasagna noodles according to the package instructions and drain them.
  3. Mix the ricotta cheese with the egg and Italian seasoning in a small bowl.
  4. Spread a thin layer of meat sauce at the bottom of a baking dish.
  5. Arrange a layer of noodles over the sauce.
  6. Spread a layer of the ricotta mixture over the noodles, then sprinkle with mozzarella and parmesan.
  7. Repeat the layers of sauce, noodles, and cheese until all ingredients are used, ending with cheese on top.
  8. Cover with foil and bake for 25 minutes, then remove the foil and bake for another 25 minutes until bubbling.
